Use the solidcraft MCP tools. /lab/work/stand.solidcraft is a 3D-printable phone stand: a phone stands in its slot, leaning back. A viewer thinks it will tip over with a phone in it. Make sure it does not tip over with this phone when the stand is 3D printed with PrusaSlicer's 0.20mm SPEED preset (2 walls, 15% infill, PLA). The phone is 150 x 72 x 9 mm and weighs 210 g; it stands in the slot on its long edge (portrait). Keep it a phone stand that holds this phone. When you are done, save the design to /lab/work/stand.solidcraft and export only the stand (not any phone body you may add to check it) as STL to /lab/work/stand.stl. Then report what you changed and the numbers you relied on.
